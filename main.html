Ooty


Ooty, also known as the "Queen of Hill Stations," is a beautiful tourist destination in Tamil Nadu. It is famous for its pleasant climate, lush green hills, tea plantations, and scenic landscapes.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Ooty Lake - Enjoy boating and scenic views.
  2. Botanical Garden - Home to a wide variety of plants and flowers.
  3. Doddabetta Peak - The highest peak in the Nilgiris.
  4. Tea Museum - Learn about tea production and history.
  5. Nilgiri Mountain Railway - A UNESCO-listed heritage train ride.

Must-Try Foods

  • Ooty Varkey - A famous local snack.
  • Homemade Chocolates - Popular among tourists.
  • Hot Masala Tea - Made from fresh Nilgiri tea leaves.
  • Vegetable Biryani - A flavorful local favorite.
  • Fresh Corn - Commonly sold at tourist spots.
