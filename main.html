Goa

Goa is one of the beautiful place in indian and that has been good beach to relax.Anyone can enjoy the water sports, food & nightlife

  1. Baga Beach - Famous for water sports
  2. Calangute Beach - Known as for 'Queen of Beaches'
  3. Fort Aguada - A historic fort
  4. Dudhsagar Waterfalls - One of the Tallest waterfalls in India
  5. Basilica - UNESCO world heritage church
  • Goan Fish - Traditional fish curry
  • Prawn balcho- Famous prawn pickle
  • Chicken cafreal- Delicious Green masala Chicken
  • Bebina - Famous Dessert
  • Goan Sausage- Spicy Sausage
