Goa


Goa is one of the most popular holiday destinations in India. It is famous for its beautiful beaches, vibrant nightlife, Portuguese heritage, and delicious seafood. Tourists from all over the world visit Goa to relax, explore historic churches, and enjoy water sports.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Baga Beach – Famous for water sports and nightlife.
  2. Basilica of Bom Jesus – A historic UNESCO World Heritage church.
  3. Dudhsagar Waterfalls – A stunning four-tiered waterfall.
  4. Fort Aguada – A 17th-century Portuguese fort with sea views.
  5. Anjuna Beach – Known for its flea market and sunset views.

Must-Try Foods

  • Goan Fish Curry – A spicy and tangy seafood dish.
  • Prawn Balchão – A traditional spicy prawn pickle.
  • Chicken Xacuti – A rich coconut-based curry.
  • Bebinca – A famous layered Goan dessert.
  • Pork Vindaloo – A hot and flavorful Goan specialty.
