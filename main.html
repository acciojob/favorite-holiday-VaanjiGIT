Goa


Goa is one of the most popular holiday destinations in India, known for its beautiful beaches, vibrant nightlife, and rich Portuguese heritage. It offers a perfect mix of relaxation, adventure, and cultural experiences.

Top 5 Attraction

  1. Baga Beach – Famous for its lively atmosphere and water sports.
  2. Calangute Beach – Known as the "Queen of Beaches".
  3. Fort Aguada – A historic fort with stunning sea views.
  4. Dudhsagar Waterfalls – A breathtaking natural wonder.
  5. Basilica of Bom Jesus – A UNESCO site with rich history.

Must Try Foods

  • Goan Fish Curry – A spicy and tangy delicacy.
  • Prawn Balchão – A flavorful pickle-style dish.
  • Bebinca – A traditional layered dessert.
  • Chicken Cafreal – A spicy green masala dish.
  • Feni – A local alcoholic beverage.
