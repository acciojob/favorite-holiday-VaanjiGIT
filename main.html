Goa


Goa is one of the most beautiful holiday destinations in India. It is famous for its stunning beaches, vibrant nightlife, Portuguese heritage, and delicious seafood. Tourists from all over the world visit Goa to relax, enjoy water sports, and explore its unique culture.

  1. Baga Beach – Famous for water sports and nightlife.
  2. Calangute Beach – Known as the "Queen of Beaches".
  3. Fort Aguada – A historic Portuguese fort with amazing sea views.
  4. Dudhsagar Waterfalls – One of the tallest waterfalls in India.
  5. Basilica of Bom Jesus – A UNESCO World Heritage church.
  • Goan Fish Curry – A traditional spicy seafood dish.
  • Prawn Balchão – A flavorful prawn pickle dish.
  • Chicken Cafreal – A delicious green masala chicken dish.
  • Bebinca – A famous Goan layered dessert.
  • Goan Sausage – A spicy Portuguese-inspired sausage.
